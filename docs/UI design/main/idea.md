"Duolingo for the Bible" experience, playful, gamified interface with a vibrant color palette, progress tracking, and interactive lessons.

Here is a proposed list of screens to build out the core experience:

**Learner Dashboard (Home):** A central hub showing the "Bible Path" (like Duolingo's tree), current streak, daily goals, and unlocked achievements.

**Lesson Interface:** An interactive screen where users engage with specific verses. It will feature multiple-choice questions, verse-scrambles, and the ability to toggle between translations (KJV, ESV, etc.) to understand nuances.

**Verse Library & Comparison:** A research-focused view where users can deep-dive into specific chapters, compare translations side-by-side, and save "Mastered" verses.

**Leaderboard & Community:** A social screen showing weekly leagues, friends' progress, and community challenges (e.g., "The Romans Roadmap").

**Profile & Milestones:** A personal space for users to see their "Bible IQ," earned badges (e.g., "Pentateuch Pro"), and overall mastery percentage.
Design Direction:

**Palette:** Friendly and "colorful" as requested—think bright blues, greens, and yellows to keep the mood light and encouraging.

**Typography:** Clean, highly readable sans-serif for lessons, paired with a modern serif for the actual Bible text to give it a sense of importance.

**Interactions:** Micro-animations for correct answers, celebratory progress bars, and tactile-feeling buttons.
