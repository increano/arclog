
To ensure a seamless and motivating start for your BibleQuest users, I suggest building out the following onboarding and authentication flow. 

This sequence is designed to mirror the "Duolingo style," prioritizing low-friction entry and immediate value.

*Proposed Onboarding & Auth Flow*

**Welcome & Value Prop:** A vibrant splash screen introducing the "Master the Word" mission with a clear "Get Started" call to action.

**Personalized Goal Setting:** A quick survey asking: "Why are you learning?" (e.g., Personal Growth, Academic Study, Daily Devotion) and "What is your daily goal?" (5, 10, or 20 minutes).

**Placement "Quiz":** A very simple interaction (like the Lesson Interface) that lets the user experience the app immediately before they even create an account.

**Account Creation (The Soft Wall):** After the first "mini-lesson," users are prompted to save their progress by creating an account. This screen will feature Social Sign-On (Google/Apple) for one-tap access.

**Translation Preference:** A final onboarding step where users select their "Default Translation" (KJV, ESV, NIV, etc.) to customize their future lessons.

**Login Screen:** A clean, focused entry point for returning "Disciples" to get back into their journey.
Design Direction

**Encouraging Tone:** Use the playful colors and rounded buttons from your Lumina Scriptura system.

**Progressive Disclosure:** Don't ask for too much info upfront; keep the momentum moving toward that first lesson.
